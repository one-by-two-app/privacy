One by Two: privacy policy

Updated 23 November 2026

One by Two collects no information about you.

  • No accounts. You never sign up or sign in.
  • No data leaves your phone. The app has no servers. Your songs, recordings and settings are kept only on your iPhone.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no ads. The app doesn't record how you use it, and shares nothing with anyone.
  • The microphone is used only when you tap a microphone pad to record your voice over your loops. The recording stays on your phone in the song it belongs to.
  • Sending a song. When you choose to send a song (with AirDrop, Messages, Files and so on), the whole song goes as one file, including any voice recordings and the song's notes. It goes only to the person or place you choose, through Apple's own sharing.
  • Beta testing. If you test the app through Apple's TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and any feedback you choose to send with the developer, under Apple's own privacy policy. One by Two adds nothing to that.

If this ever changes, this page will say so first.

Contact: while the app is in testing, use "Share Beta Feedback" in TestFlight.
